## Review Focus

- Multipart trailers/duplicate parts, forged MIME, split UTF-8 and late invalid bytes must not commit objects or rows.
- Cancellation, uncertain database commit, and failed cleanup must not delete a potentially referenced object or silently lose pending cleanup information.
- Process/delete and process/process concurrency must not publish text for a deleted document or duplicate READY writes.
- A late upload/process/download after organization/session change must not affect another context or save unauthorized data.
- Empty organization onboarding must create an organization with trusted server-side admin identity and enter the work area; registration remains separate from organization creation.
